Two things a team needs from social.
A gate before anything goes out and a place to answer the people
who write back.
This video covers the approval workflow, queue on approval,
engage and account health.
Settings, then People. At the top, social posts need an admin's
approval. Turn it on and a post by a non -admin is held until an
admin approves it. Nothing unapproved can be sent, scheduled,
queued, or dragged onto the calendar.
That is enforced on the server, not just in the interface.
Under full autonomy, the autopilot's own posts wait here too.
Publishing an article is an admin's act regardless.
Held posts appear in the inbox and under publish, approvals.
Approve or request changes with a note for the author.
A post with changes requested goes back to its author and cannot
be sent until it is approved again.
One trap to know. An approved draft that was never queued will
never send. Queue on approval under settings, automation collapses the two.
The moment an admin approves, the post takes the next free slot.
Turn it on with a team and approving becomes the last human act.
Distribute then engage.
Comments, direct messages and reviews across every account this
workspace can read in one place.
Answer people the day they write.
The one hard deadline in the whole app lives here.
Facebook and Instagram only accept a direct message reply within
24 hours of the person's message.
