## s01 · title (8.4s voice, 10.3s scene, starts 0s)

Here is one article, end to end. From an idea the engine found, to a live page on your website, with a person at every gate.

## s02 · screen · 01 · Ideas (18.0s voice, 19.9s scene, starts 10.3s)

It starts on the Ideas board. The engine discovers article ideas grounded in your company profile, your Topics and your keyword strategy, and lands them in the Discovered column. You approve the ones worth writing and reject the rest. Only approved ideas are drafted.

## s03 · screen · 02 · The sweep drafts it (17.0s voice, 18.9s scene, starts 30.2s)

Every thirty minutes the sweep picks up approved ideas, inside the weekly target you set. It writes the article from your brand context, tone and expert profiles, then moves it across the board: drafting, in review, final approval, published.

## s04 · screen · 03 · The article (17.1s voice, 19s scene, starts 49.1s)

Open it and everything is already there. A focus keyword, meta title, meta description and slug. A featured image and a branded social preview, generated from your brand kit, or uploaded. Metadata you write by hand is never overwritten.

## s05 · screen · 04 · The checklist (20.4s voice, 22.3s scene, starts 68.1s)

The checklist shows every gate and whether it passes. Metadata, slug, focus keyword, alt text on every image, no unsourced claims, every citation verified, a branded preview image. Auto-review fixes what it can. Whatever it cannot fix parks in your Inbox, with the action on the card.

## s06 · screen · 05 · Publish (19.8s voice, 21.7s scene, starts 90.4s)

At final approval it waits for your publish day, in your timezone. Or set a date for this one article. Or publish it right now. WordPress connected directly. No WordPress? Download a self-contained HTML file, add it to any site, and mark the article as published with the live link.
