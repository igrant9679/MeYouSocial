# MeYouSocial Publisher in two minutes — narration

_Voice: Kokoro am_michael. Total 139.8s._

## s01 · title (8.4s voice, 10.3s scene, starts 0s)

Most content tools hand you a blank page and a calendar. MeYouSocial Publisher hands you a machine, with a person at the gates.

## s02 · screen · 01 · The Inbox (17.8s voice, 19.7s scene, starts 10.3s)

Every thirty minutes a sweep runs. It discovers ideas, drafts the ones you approved, gives each article its images and SEO, sources every claim, and advances what passes. Whatever it cannot fix waits here, in your Inbox, with the action on the card.

## s03 · screen · 02 · One loop (13.5s voice, 15.4s scene, starts 30s)

The whole interface is one loop. Research, Ideas, Drafts, Review, Publish, Distribute, Measure. The Inbox above it shows exactly what is waiting on you. Setup beneath it holds the dials.

## s04 · screen · 03 · Ideas (12.7s voice, 14.6s scene, starts 45.4s)

Ideas hang off your Topics, on one board for articles, social posts and video. The engine discovers them. You approve them. Only approved ideas are drafted, and approving is your act.

## s05 · screen · 04 · Drafts (15.2s voice, 17.1s scene, starts 60s)

Drafts arrive grounded in your brand and your experts, with a focus keyword, meta title, description and slug filled in, a featured image, a branded social preview, and a checklist that shows every gate and whether it passes.

## s06 · screen · 05 · Review (17.0s voice, 18.9s scene, starts 77.1s)

Review is what still needs a person. A claim with no source: verify it with a link, or drop it. An image that needs your eye. A question only you can answer, asked once and banked to the expert profile. Every clearing act moves the article at once.

## s07 · screen · 06 · Publish & Distribute (14.8s voice, 16.7s scene, starts 96s)

Publish sends the article to your website on your publish day, or right now with one click. Distribute fans one post out to fifteen networks through a single connection, on posting slots you set once, in your own timezone.

## s08 · screen · 07 · Measure (14.6s voice, 16.5s scene, starts 112.7s)

And Measure pulls the results back in. Search impressions and clicks, traffic, engagement by network, and the best time to post. Measured numbers only. A dash means not measured. Never zero, and never a guess.

## s09 · close (8.3s voice, 10.6s scene, starts 129.2s)

MeYouSocial Publisher. One loop from research to results, mostly on its own. Your job is decisions, not production.
