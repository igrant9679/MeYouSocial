# Clearing the gates — narration

_Voice: Kokoro am_michael. Total 165.3s._

## s01 · title (14.8s voice, 16.7s scene, starts 0s)

Review is what still needs a person after the engine has done what it can. Everything here is in the Inbox, each card with its own button. This video shows how to clear each kind of hold, and what happens the moment you do.

## s02 · screen · 01 · Questions only you can answer (22.2s voice, 24.1s scene, starts 16.7s)

Questions only you can answer sit at the top. Each names the article and the sentence that needs your knowledge. Answer inline, in your own words. The answer is woven into the article and banked to the expert profile, so it is asked once. Answer all clears the card in one go. Dismiss, with an optional why, if the question does not apply.

## s03 · screen · 02 · Claims with no source (25.5s voice, 27.4s scene, starts 40.8s)

Claims with no source. The writer marked a factual claim and live search could not find a source an independent judge agreed with. Two buttons. Verify: paste a link that genuinely supports the sentence. Drop: the marker and the record go, and you edit the sentence in the article if it should go too. A claim needs a live-search key to be sourced automatically; give it one sweep, then decide.

## s04 · screen · 03 · Images that need your eye (20.2s voice, 22.1s scene, starts 68.2s)

Images that need your eye. The vision model found cut-off text, a wrong brand name or a glitch, and after two failed renders it stopped spending. Approve the image as it is, or open the article and pick or upload another. The brake means auto-review has already spent up to three renders on it.

## s05 · screen · 04 · Held articles (20.9s voice, 22.8s scene, starts 90.3s)

Held articles name the failing check on the card. Open the article and the Gates sidebar links to the tab where you fix it. An admin who wants it out regardless has Advance anyway: an optional reason, then it moves to final approval now, and who, when and why are recorded on the article and in the audit log.

## s06 · screen · 05 · Approvals and Audit (19.4s voice, 21.3s scene, starts 113.1s)

Two more tabs under Publish. Approvals lists social posts waiting for an admin when the approval workflow is on: approve, or request changes with a note for the author. Audit scans the archive and recommends what to refresh, merge or retire, so it never goes stale. Run it monthly.

## s07 · screen · 06 · What happens when you clear (20.3s voice, 22.2s scene, starts 134.4s)

Every clearing act moves the article at once. Answer a question, verify or drop a claim, approve an image, and if that was the last thing holding it, the article advances to final approval immediately. Nothing waits for the next sweep. Work the Inbox top to bottom until the header count reads zero.

## s08 · close (6.4s voice, 8.7s scene, starts 156.6s)

Answer, verify or drop, approve or replace, and advance when you must. Next: publishing.
