Brand holds the things every generation should obey, so you set
them once instead of restating them in each prompt.
Your tone, your colors and logo, your company facts, your
topics, your guardrails, and your experts.
If output keeps coming back sounding wrong, this is the page to
fix, not the prompt.
Tone lives in brand, tone and motifs as the seven motifs.
Visionary, competitive, succinct, sincere, exclusive, social and
informative. Each is an editable version style directive.
Every piece resolves a weighted blend of them.
The strongest weight sets structure and voice.
The rest color the intro and the call to action.
Change a motif and every future generation changes with it
without touching a prompt.
On the same page, the brand kit,
colors, logo, image dimensions, and the asset policy, which
decides whether AI may generate imagery at all.
Either way, AI images land as pending for review.
The require images to publish gate lives here too.
If your workspace has no real image key yet, turn that gate off,
or it will hold every article behind a placeholder.
Brand context is what the AI reads before it writes.
Your differentiators, your products, and what each does, brand
documents, personas. Nothing here is AI generated on purpose.
An invented differentiator would be repeated as fact in every
article afterwards.
Write these yourself plainly.
Guardrails are the rules it must obey, and experts are the
subject matter profiles the writing quotes.
Experts are the people whose knowledge the writing draws on.
When an article needs something only you know, the question is
asked once in the inbox.
Your answer, in your own words, is woven into the article and
banked to the expert profile.
These fields deliberately have no draft with AI button.
An answer must be a real person's own words, or the profile
trained on it is poisoned.
Topics live under ideas. A topic is a theme this company
publishes about. It is the one tag that spans every content
surface. Article ideas, social posts, and video ideas all carry the same
topic, which is what lets reports and insights group everything
on a theme instead of showing six unrelated lists.
The engine ideates per topic, so every idea it writes carries
one. Deleting a topic only clears the tag.
It never deletes content.
Open a topic and you see everything that hangs off it in every
format and later under measure, topics, what each topic earned
per format, measured or a dash with the reason.
One reminder, brand and topics are per company.
Check the workspace switcher before editing because it is easy
to change the wrong company's identity when you run more than
one. Motifs for tone, brand context for facts, experts for knowledge,
topics for the spine.
