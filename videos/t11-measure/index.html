Measure pulls the results back in, and it shows measured numbers
only. This video covers connecting analytics once, reading Search,
Traffic and Social performance, the best time to post, and the
Reports and Insights pages.
Settings, then Analytics.
Enter the Search Console site and the GA4 property, and grant
the service account on both: as a user in Search Console, as a
Viewer in GA4. The page runs a live probe and tells you which
side is missing. A disabled API and a missing grant both return
permission denied; the probe separates them.
Until this is done, Measure shows dashes.
Measure's overview shows search impressions and clicks by week,
and tracked posts with their positions and deltas.
Blog analytics adds traffic from GA4.
A blank is never a zero. A blank means we do not know; a zero
means we measured nothing happening.
Every dash comes with the reason it is a dash, and every figure
carries its sample size and source.
Measure, then Social performance: engagement by network, synced
on a schedule or on demand.
Read the window carefully. It means posts sent in that window,
not engagement earned in it, because networks report lifetime
totals per post. The evidence line under each figure says so.
Best time to post is measured from real engagement, and it stays
silent below its sample size rather than guessing.
Reports holds ten stock reports: Traffic overview, Content
performance, Keyword rankings, Pipeline velocity, Autopilot
operations, Editorial compliance, Voice and motifs, Social
distribution, Video production and Content audit, plus any
custom reports you build.
Open one and Customize adds or removes blocks, reorders them,
and sets the date range. Every report has a PDF button;
blocks without data say so instead of drawing a curve.
Insights measures two different things and shows them apart on
purpose: your pipeline, what you made and what stalled, which
the app knows exactly; and performance, which it knows only as
well as your connected analytics allow.
Recommendations are deterministic rules that refuse to fire
below a usable sample. And Measure, Topics, shows what each
Topic earned per format. Read it monthly, and decide what to do
more of. Connect once, read weekly, decide monthly, and trust the dashes.
