Measure pulls the results back in and it shows measured numbers
only. This video covers connecting analytics once, reading search,
traffic and social performance, the best time to post, and the
reports and insights pages.
Settings, then Analytics.
Enter the Search Console site in the G of 4 property and grant
the service account on both, as a user in Search Console, as a
viewer in G of 4. The page runs a live probe and tells you which
side is missing. A disabled, a PI, and a missing grant both
return permission denied. The probe separates them.
Until this is done, measure shows dashes.
Measures Overview shows search impressions and clicks by week,
and tracked posts with their positions and deltas.
Blog Analytics adds traffic from G of 4.
A blank is never a zero. A blank means we do not know.
A zero means we measured nothing happening.
Every dash comes with the reason it is a dash, and every figure
carries its sample size and source.
Measure then social performance.
Engagement by network synced on a schedule or on demand.
Read the window carefully. It means posts sent in that window,
not engagement earned in it, because networks report lifetime
totals per post. The evidence line under each figure says so.
Best time to post is measured from real engagement and it stays
silent below its sample size rather than guessing.
Reports holds 10 stock reports.
Traffic overview content performance, keyword rankings, pipeline
velocity, autopilot operations, editorial compliance, voice and
motifs, social distribution, video production and content audit,
plus any custom reports you build.
Open one and customize ads or removes blocks, reorders them and
sets the date range. Every report has a PDF button.
Blocks without data say so instead of drawing a curve.
Insights measures two different things and shows them apart on
purpose. Your pipeline, what you made and what stalled, which
the app knows exactly. And performance, which it knows only as
well as your connected analytics allow.
