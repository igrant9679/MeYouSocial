## s02 · screen · 01 · Ask (32.5s voice, 34.4s scene, starts 15.2s)

Press Ask, or Control and slash, on any page. The assistant knows which page you are on and reads everything: the pipeline, the Inbox, ideas, articles and their checks, social posts, keywords, Topics, connections, dials, results, the live web, and the app's own guide. And it does the work: finds and approves ideas, drafts articles, writes SEO, runs Optimize, verifies claims, queues and sends posts, publishes, changes the dials. Ask what should I do next and it ranks the moves and offers to do each one.

## s03 · screen · 02 · It asks first (33.9s voice, 35.8s scene, starts 49.6s)

Anything outward-facing or hard to undo is proposed first: publishing, sending, queueing, approving, an override, a setting, an invitation. It says I am about to, and asks go ahead. It runs only when you say yes, and the proposal waits on the thread until you answer. Admin-only acts refuse for an editor, exactly as the page would. The steps it took sit under every reply. It will not enter API keys, delete a workspace or an account, or send email; and with no working AI key it refuses the turn rather than guessing.

## s04 · screen · 03 · People and roles (29.5s voice, 31.4s scene, starts 85.4s)

Settings, then People. Invite by email with a role. Admins approve, publish, send and configure. Editors write, draft, answer and propose. Viewers read. Publishing an article and approving a social post are always an admin's act. Revoke suspends a membership and keeps its history; Remove deletes it. You cannot remove the last active admin; promote someone first. There is no billing anywhere: access is roles, plus optional soft limits under Usage.

## s05 · screen · 04 · Workspaces, digest, audit (39.8s voice, 41.7s scene, starts 116.8s)

Each company is a sealed workspace: keys, accounts, voice, content, slots and dials are its own, and the switcher at the top left decides whose everything the pages show. Under Settings, Workspace, an accent colour and logo re-tint the app for your members only: co-branded, never white-labelled. Admins get one morning digest on the days something needs them, and everyone can turn it off for themselves under Notifications. And an audit log records everything: what the autopilot did and why, what a person clicked, what the assistant ran and with which arguments, and every deletion, named before it disappears.

## s06 · screen · 05 · When something looks wrong (47.4s voice, 49.3s scene, starts 158.5s)

When something looks wrong, it is usually one of seven things. Output reads generic or mentions mock: no working AI key for this workspace. Nothing is being drafted: the Approved column is empty, the target or budget is reached, drafting is on manual, or global pause is on. An article is held with nothing to act on: the card names the check; a claim needs a live-search key. At final approval but never publishes: not the day, no WordPress, or publishing not on auto. Social never sends: no slots, awaiting approval, approved but never queued, or a broken account. Measure shows dashes: analytics not connected. A button does nothing after an update: reload the tab. And always: trust the dashes.
