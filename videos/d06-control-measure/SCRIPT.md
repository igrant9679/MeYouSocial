## s01 · title (6.5s voice, 8.4s scene, starts 0s)

An engine that runs on its own needs dials you trust, and numbers you can believe. Here are both.

## s02 · screen · 01 · Function modes (19.9s voice, 21.8s scene, starts 8.4s)

Four functions, each with its own mode. Ideation, drafting, social and publishing. Manual means the AI acts only when you click. Assisted means it runs and queues at a human checkpoint. Auto means unattended. Start low, and raise a dial once you trust what arrives at review.

## s03 · screen · 02 · One switch, one brake (24.3s voice, 26.2s scene, starts 30.2s)

Full autonomy is one switch. It sets every function to auto, turns queue-on-approval on, and remembers what you had, so switching it off restores your previous dials exactly. Under it, the app presses the review buttons a person would. It does not lower what they check. Global pause is the emergency brake: every AI action stops, manual clicks included.

## s04 · screen · 03 · Roles and workspaces (23.4s voice, 25.3s scene, starts 56.4s)

Admins approve, publish, send and configure. Editors write, draft, answer and propose. Viewers read. Publishing an article and approving a social post are always an admin's act. And every company is its own sealed workspace: keys, accounts, voice, content, slots and dials are its own. Co-branded for agencies, never white-labelled.

## s05 · screen · 04 · The audit log (15.1s voice, 17s scene, starts 81.7s)

An audit log records everything. What the autopilot did and why, what a person clicked, what the assistant ran and with which arguments. The morning digest lists what needs you, and a quiet morning sends nothing at all.

## s06 · screen · 05 · Measure (21.5s voice, 23.4s scene, starts 98.7s)

On the other end, Measure. Search impressions and clicks by week from Search Console. Traffic from Google Analytics. Social performance by network. The best time to post, measured from real engagement, that stays silent below its sample size rather than guessing. Reports built from your own data, exported as PDF.
