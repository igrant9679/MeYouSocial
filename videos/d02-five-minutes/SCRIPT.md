# Five minutes a day — narration

_Voice: Kokoro am_michael. Total 115s._

## s01 · title (8.6s voice, 10.5s scene, starts 0s)

Most days, running MeYouSocial Publisher is reading one email, or opening one page. The routine is a check, not a shift.

## s02 · screen · 01 · The digest (15.4s voice, 17.3s scene, starts 10.5s)

If something urgent is waiting, the workspace's admins get one morning digest email listing it, one link per item. A quiet morning sends nothing, on purpose. No email and a green Inbox means genuinely nothing to do.

## s03 · screen · 02 · Work the Inbox (19.3s voice, 21.2s scene, starts 27.8s)

Work the Inbox top to bottom. Approve or request changes on a post. Answer a question in your own words; it is saved to the expert profile and asked once. Verify a claim with a real link, or drop it. Approve or replace a held image. The header count should reach zero.

## s04 · screen · 03 · Engage (17.7s voice, 19.6s scene, starts 49s)

Answer people the day they write. Distribute, then Engage, holds comments, direct messages and reviews in one place. The one hard deadline in the whole app is here: Facebook and Instagram only accept a direct message reply within twenty-four hours.

## s05 · screen · 04 · Ask (14.3s voice, 16.2s scene, starts 68.6s)

If you would rather not hunt, ask. The Ask button sits on every page. Say what should I do next, and the assistant reads the Inbox, the pipeline, the connections and the dials, ranks the moves, and offers to do each one.

## s06 · screen · 05 · It asks first (20.2s voice, 22.1s scene, starts 84.8s)

It can do nearly everything you can do here. Anything outward-facing or hard to undo is proposed first, as I am about to, go ahead? It runs only when you say yes. Every action lands in the audit log. And it will never enter an API key, delete a workspace, or send an email on its own.

## s07 · close (5.8s voice, 8.1s scene, starts 106.9s)

Five minutes a day. MeYouSocial Publisher. Your job is decisions, not production.
