> Drafts, then Articles, is the board. A post moves along one track: Drafting, being written. In review, the AI has finished and parked it; this is where a human reads it. Final approval, reviewed and queued to go out. Published, live. The two middle stages are separate on purpose: automation may move a post into review, but only full autonomy publishes from approval, and even then the gates re-run first.

> Optimize runs an analysis pass for expertise and trust signals, entity coverage and content gaps, and sorts what it finds into three kinds. Mechanical findings apply themselves. Knowledge findings ask you a question only you can answer, and your answer is banked to the expert profile. Strategic findings ask for a decision: apply, or dismiss with a reason. Nothing here rewrites your article without your say.
