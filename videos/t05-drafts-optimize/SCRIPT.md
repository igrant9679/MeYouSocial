# Reading a draft, and Optimize — narration

_Voice: Kokoro am_michael. Total 154.9s._

## s01 · title (13.1s voice, 15s scene, starts 0s)

Once a week, read what was drafted: words, pictures and SEO together. This video walks the article page tab by tab, then the Optimize pass and the three kinds of finding it produces.

## s02 · screen · 01 · The board (26.3s voice, 28.2s scene, starts 15s)

Drafts, then Articles, is the board. A post moves along one track: Drafting, being written. In review, the AI has finished and parked it; this is where a human reads it. Final approval, reviewed and queued to go out. Published, live. The two middle stages are separate on purpose: automation may move a post into review, but only full autonomy publishes from approval, and even then the gates re-run first.

## s03 · screen · 02 · Write (19.1s voice, 21s scene, starts 43.2s)

Open a card and the editor has five tabs. Write holds the title, the motif blend this article uses, the body, and its versions, so you can always go back. Every article is written from your brand context, tone and expert profiles, and it cites its sources as it goes.

## s04 · screen · 03 · SEO and images (23.6s voice, 25.5s scene, starts 64.2s)

The SEO panel carries the focus keyword, meta title, meta description and slug, filled in by the engine. Anything you write by hand here is never overwritten. Assets holds the featured image and the branded social preview at your workspace dimensions. AI images land as pending for review, and the vision model has already looked at each one.

## s05 · screen · 04 · The Gates sidebar (23.7s voice, 25.6s scene, starts 89.7s)

The Gates sidebar on the right shows the publish contract from every tab. SEO meta present. All citations verified. No needs-source markers left. Descriptive link text. An approved featured image and a branded preview image. Each failing gate links to the tab where you fix it. Admins can relax the image requirement under Brand, Asset policy.

## s06 · screen · 05 · Optimize (27.0s voice, 28.9s scene, starts 115.3s)

Optimize runs an analysis pass for expertise and trust signals, entity coverage and content gaps, and sorts what it finds into three kinds. Mechanical findings apply themselves. Knowledge findings ask you a question only you can answer, and your answer is banked to the expert profile. Strategic findings ask for a decision: apply, or dismiss with a reason. Nothing here rewrites your article without your say.

## s07 · close (8.4s voice, 10.7s scene, starts 144.2s)

Read the draft, check the gates, answer the knowledge cards, decide the strategic ones. Next: clearing the gates from the Inbox.
