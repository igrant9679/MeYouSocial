This video walks the article page tab by tab, then the Optimize
pass and the three kinds of finding it produces.
Drafts, then Articles, is the board.
A post moves along one track: Drafting, being written.
In review, the AI has finished and parked it; this is where a
human reads it. Final approval, reviewed and queued to go out.
Published, live. The two middle stages are separate on purpose:
automation may move a post into review, but only full autonomy
publishes from approval, and even then the gates re-run first.
Open a card and the editor has five tabs.
Write holds the title, the motif blend this article uses, the
body, and its versions, so you can always go back.
Every article is written from your brand context,
tone and expert profiles, and it cites its sources as it goes.
The SEO panel carries the focus keyword, meta title, meta
description and slug, filled in by the engine.
Anything you write by hand here is never overwritten.
Assets holds the featured image and the branded social preview
at your workspace dimensions.
AI images land as pending for review, and the vision model has
already looked at each one.
The Gates sidebar on the right shows the publish contract from
every tab. SEO meta present. All citations verified.
No needs-source markers left.
Descriptive link text. An approved featured image and a branded
preview image. Each failing gate links to the tab where you fix
it. Admins can relax the image requirement under Brand, Asset
policy. Optimize runs an analysis pass for expertise and trust signals,
entity coverage and content gaps, and sorts what it finds into
three kinds.
Mechanical findings apply themselves.
Knowledge findings ask you a question only you can answer, and
your answer is banked to the expert profile.
Strategic findings ask for a decision: apply, or dismiss with a
reason. Nothing here rewrites your article without your say.
Read the draft, check the gates, answer the knowledge cards,
decide the strategic ones.
