Research is where raw material comes from, and Ideas is where
you decide what gets written.
This video covers both: reading competitor outliers in Intel,
and the weekly triage on the Ideas board.
Intel indexes public YouTube channels by handle or keyword.
Every video gets an outlier score: its views divided by the
average of the surrounding videos on the same channel.
Five times or more is exceptional.
Two to five is strong. Under one is below average.
And a dash means not yet measured, never estimated.
Open a channel to see its videos ranked by outlier score.
Bookmarks keep the videos worth returning to.
Competitors is the set each channel's outliers are measured
against. Search accepts tokens too:
subs greater than one hundred thousand, velocity greater than
five, format short. And Ask about this channel, or Ask about
this video, opens the assistant with the stats, outliers and
transcript already loaded, so you can work out why something
performed and how to remix it.
When a strong outlier fits one of your Topics, Make it an idea
turns it into an article idea in one click.
It lands on the Ideas board in the Discovered column, tagged to
the Topic, ready for the same triage as everything else.
The Ideas board is one board for article, social and video
ideas, four columns:
Discovered, Approved, Drafted, Rejected.
Topics are the lanes.
Each card carries a format chip and a priority score, and
Recompute priorities shows every score's working.
Discover article ideas grounds new ones in your company profile,
Topics and keyword strategy.
Generate video ideas does the same per channel.
Once a week, the day before your publish day, triage the
Discovered column. Approve the ones worth writing, reject the
rest. Approved ideas are what the autopilot drafts from, inside your
weekly target. An empty Approved column means no new articles,
however high the dial. Keep three to five approved.
Only approved ideas are drafted, and approving is your act.
Under Ideas, Keywords holds a tiered keyword strategy,
discovered by the app or added by hand.
It drives idea priority and each article's focus keyword.
Add the terms you actually want to rank for, and the board's
scores follow. Outliers in, approved ideas out, and the engine writes only what
you said yes to. Next: reading a draft, and Optimize.
