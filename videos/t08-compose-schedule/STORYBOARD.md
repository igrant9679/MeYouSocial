> Distribute, then Compose. Pick the accounts under Post to; one post fans out to every selected account. Write once. Where a network needs its own version, Customize overrides the text or the image for that network alone. Character counts run against each network's real limit, and image requirements are enforced before you send. Instagram, Pinterest and YouTube cannot take a text-only post.

> Three ways to send. Queue drops the post into the next free posting slot. Schedule sends it once, at a time you pick. Post now sends immediately. Draft with AI on the text box writes a first version from what the workspace knows and proposes it; nothing touches your text until you accept.

> Settings, then Schedule. Set the workspace timezone and the posting slots: recurring send times, a weekday and a minute. They are stored as wall clock, so nine o'clock stays nine o'clock through daylight-saving changes. Without slots, every post needs a hand-picked date, and Queue has nowhere to put anything. Slot categories let a rule pick which slot a kind of post takes; anything without a match falls back to a general slot, so nothing strands.

> Two dials that run on their own. Auto-generated posts come from your Topics, up to a weekly quota you choose under Settings, Automation, optionally under a named campaign with its own tracking parameter. Every outbound link is tagged. And evergreen recycling clones a post that performed into free slots after its cooldown. It is opt-in, and a recycled post is never itself recycled.
