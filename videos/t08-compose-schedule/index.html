Distribute is social, on a schedule you set once.
This video covers composing a post, the three ways to send it,
posting slots and the timezone, the calendar,
campaigns, and evergreen recycling.
Distribute, then Compose.
Pick the accounts under Post to; one post fans out to every
selected account. Write once.
Where a network needs its own version, Customize overrides the
text or the image for that network alone.
Character counts run against each network's real limit, and
image requirements are enforced before you send.
Instagram, Pinterest and YouTube cannot take a text-only post.
Three ways to send. Queue drops the post into the next free
posting slot.
Schedule sends it once, at a time you pick.
Post now sends immediately.
Draft with AI on the text box writes a first version from what
the workspace knows and proposes it; nothing touches your text
until you accept.
Settings, then Schedule.
Set the workspace timezone and the posting slots: recurring send
times, a weekday and a minute.
They are stored as wall clock, so nine o'clock stays nine
o'clock through daylight-saving changes.
Without slots, every post needs a hand-picked date, and Queue
has nowhere to put anything.
Slot categories let a rule pick which slot a kind of post takes;
anything without a match falls back to a general slot, so
nothing strands. Distribute, then Calendar. The month view shows what is queued
and scheduled; switch to Week for the detail.
Drag a post to another day to reschedule it.
The queue list below is in your posting
timezone. Recently published shows what went out, with a
separate status per network, and any failed network called out.
Two dials that run on their own.
Auto-generated posts come from your Topics, up to a weekly quota
you choose under Settings, Automation,
optionally under a named campaign with its own tracking
parameter. Every outbound link is tagged.
And evergreen recycling clones a post that performed into free
slots after its cooldown. It is opt-in, and a recycled post is
never itself recycled.
Compose once, set the slots once, and let Queue do the placing.
