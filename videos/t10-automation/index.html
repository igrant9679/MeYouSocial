Settings then Automation is where you decide how much the engine
does on its own.
This video covers the four function modes, the Targets and
Cadence, the Full Autonomy Switch, the Global Pause, Running the
Loop by Hand, and the Social Idea Gate.
Four functions, each with its own mode.
Ideation, drafting, social, and publishing.
Manual, the AI acts only when you click.
Assisted, it runs, then queues at a human checkpoint.
Auto, unattended, they are independent.
A common setup is ideation and drafting on auto.
Social, unassisted, publishing on manual.
Start low and raise a dial once you trust what arrives at
review. Targets and cadence, articles per week in the published day,
social posts per week, SEO on every draft, auto image, evergreen
recycling, and a daily generation budget.
Remember the engine only drafts approved ideas, so an empty
approved column means no new articles, however high the target.
Full autonomy is one switch.
It sets every function to auto, turns Q on approval on and
remembers what you had. So switching it off restores your
previous dials exactly.
Under it, the app presses the review buttons a person would.
It approves its own ideas, clears what it can, and only holds
what it genuinely cannot fix.
It does not lower what the gates check.
Global pause is the emergency break.
Every AI action stops, manual clicks included until you release
it. Run cycle now runs one sweep immediately.
Watch drafts and the inbox afterwards.
An idle sweep is silent by design.
Other activity like analytic syncs shows the scheduler is alive.
Every 30 minutes the sweep runs on its own.
A social post starts as an idea.
Each sweep ideates per topic, and from a published article's
angles, then a gate decides.
On auto, the engine approves its own social ideas in the same
sweep and drafts one approved idea per sweep inside the weekly
quota. On human, they wait on the ideas board for you.
A person's own ideas always wait for that person.
If nothing is being drafted, it is one of four things.
The approved column is empty.
The weekly target or the daily budget is reached.
Drafting is on manual or global pause is on.
Settings, automation says which.
