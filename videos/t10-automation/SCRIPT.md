# The autonomy dials — narration

_Voice: Kokoro am_michael. Total 172.4s._

## s01 · title (16.7s voice, 18.6s scene, starts 0s)

Settings, then Automation, is where you decide how much the engine does on its own. This video covers the four function modes, the targets and cadence, the full autonomy switch, the global pause, running the loop by hand, and the social idea gate.

## s02 · screen · 01 · Function modes (26.0s voice, 27.9s scene, starts 18.6s)

Four functions, each with its own mode: ideation, drafting, social and publishing. Manual: the AI acts only when you click. Assisted: it runs, then queues at a human checkpoint. Auto: unattended. They are independent. A common setup is ideation and drafting on auto, social on assisted, publishing on manual. Start low, and raise a dial once you trust what arrives at review.

## s03 · screen · 02 · Targets and cadence (21.1s voice, 23s scene, starts 46.5s)

Targets and cadence: articles per week and the publish day, social posts per week, SEO on every draft, auto-image, evergreen recycling, and a daily generation budget. Remember the engine only drafts approved ideas, so an empty Approved column means no new articles, however high the target.

## s04 · screen · 03 · Full autonomy (24.4s voice, 26.3s scene, starts 69.5s)

Full autonomy is one switch. It sets every function to auto, turns queue-on-approval on, and remembers what you had, so switching it off restores your previous dials exactly. Under it, the app presses the review buttons a person would: it approves its own ideas, clears what it can, and only holds what it genuinely cannot fix. It does not lower what the gates check.

## s05 · screen · 04 · Global pause and Run cycle now (23.9s voice, 25.8s scene, starts 95.8s)

Global pause is the emergency brake: every AI action stops, manual clicks included, until you release it. Run cycle now runs one sweep immediately; watch Drafts and the Inbox afterwards. An idle sweep is silent by design; other activity, like analytics syncs, shows the scheduler is alive. Every thirty minutes the sweep runs on its own.

## s06 · screen · 05 · Social ideas and the gate (24.8s voice, 26.7s scene, starts 121.6s)

A social post starts as an idea. Each sweep ideates per Topic, and from a published article's angles, then a gate decides. On auto, the engine approves its own social ideas in the same sweep and drafts one approved idea per sweep, inside the weekly quota. On human, they wait on the Ideas board for you. A person's own ideas always wait for that person.

## s07 · screen · 06 · If nothing is being drafted (15.4s voice, 17.3s scene, starts 148.3s)

If nothing is being drafted, it is one of four things. The Approved column is empty. The weekly target or the daily budget is reached. Drafting is on manual. Or global pause is on. Settings, Automation, says which.
