Waiting to go out,

Publishing an article is always an admin's act, whatever the
dials say. three ways an article goes out.
On your published day in your time zone, set under Settings,
Automation, along with the weekly target.
On a date you set for this one article, or right now with one
click, the blog calendar shows what is due and when.
With WordPress connected directly under Publish, Website,
articles publish themselves on the published day or when you
press Publish now.
Structured data and social preview tags are written for you.
Under full autonomy, the publish gates still rerun first, so a
post with an unverified citation can never go out unattended.
No WordPress, no problem. On the row, download HTML gives you a
self -contained file, metadata, open graph tags, and images
embedded. Add fragment equals one to the address for just the body for a
CMS block.
Add it to any site, then mark as published with the live link,
and the article moves to published with that URL.
Upload the open graph image to the site as well.
Crawlers ignore embedded images.
