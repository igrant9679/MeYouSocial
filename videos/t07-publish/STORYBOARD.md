> No WordPress? No problem. On the row, Download HTML gives you a self-contained file: metadata, Open Graph tags and images embedded. Add fragment equals one to the address for just the body, for a CMS block. Add it to any site, then Mark as published with the live link, and the article moves to published with that URL. Upload the Open Graph image to the site as well; crawlers ignore embedded images.
