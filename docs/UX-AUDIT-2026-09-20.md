# UX audit — MeYouSocial Publish, 2026-09-20

Walked as the owner (ADMIN) in the in-app browser at 1377×983, workspace **CommunityForce Inc.** (real
tenant — read-only; nothing submitted). ≈45 pages: every rail entry, every stage tab, Setup, Publish
Admin, Help, Notifications, the article editor, a script list, the video studio. Console read on each.
This is the **assessment only** — the owner asked for the fixes to run in a fresh session on Opus.
Owner's framing: *"very clunky and difficult to use and navigate — things are confusing and the
workflow is disjointed."* The findings below agree, and say where.

## B. Structural problems — why it feels disjointed

**B1. Two of everything.** The redesign layered the loop on top of the old modules instead of replacing them, so most things exist twice with different names:
- **Inbox ≡ Review overview** — pixel-identical (the same three "questions only you can answer" cards). Two rail entries, one screen.
- **Drafts → Articles (`/blog`) ≡ Drafts → Board (`/blog/board`)** — the same four columns (Drafting · In review · Final approval · Published) twice, one as a kanban, one as a list.
- **Settings → People ≡ Publish Admin → Users** — the same invite form and member table on two pages.
- **Settings (5 tabs) ≡ Publish Admin (10 tabs)** — two settings surfaces; API keys, Connections, Analytics, Email, Workspace, Soft limits, Usage live only under Publish Admin, while People/Automation/Schedule/Connections live under Settings. "Connections" appears in BOTH strips and they are different pages.
- **Ask ×3 on one screen** — header "Ask" button, floating "Ask" pill bottom-right, and an "Ask" drawer card at the bottom of every stage page, each with different placeholder copy.
- **Ideas board tabs Keywords/Experts** carry a "← Blog" back link that goes to *Drafts*, not Ideas; Audit (under Review) and Analytics (under Measure) do the same. The old module hierarchy leaks through the new one.

**B2. The loop is the nav, but the work isn't in the loop.** Research → Ideas → Drafts → Review → Publish → Distribute → Measure reads well, but on a real tenant most stages are either empty ("Nothing being written right now") or a copy of the Inbox. A person opening the app has 9 rail entries + Setup (4) + Help = 14 destinations, of which **three carry today's work** (Inbox, the article editor, Compose). The rest are places to look, not places to act.

**B3. Video and blog are interleaved on every board.** The Ideas board mixes 32 video ideas with article ideas across four ~200 px columns; the first card is a video idea for "Educian" (a second channel) while the workspace is CommunityForce; "Discover article ideas" and "Generate 10 video ideas" sit side by side with a channel selector between them. A blog-only tenant (which CF effectively is) sees a video product.

**B4. The assistant is presented as an API.** `/assistant` lists ~65 raw tool names in three columns (`list_ideas`, `set_publish_day`, `intel_video`…). That page should show *what you can ask*, in sentences, not the tool registry.

**B5. Density without hierarchy.** The header packs: logo/byline, workspace select, "+ Channel", three status chips, Ask, Elsie (with a badge), bell (99+), ADMIN badge — eleven controls in one 48 px bar. Stage pages then add a strip (title + Overview + tabs), a count-chip row, then the content, then the Ask drawer. On a 1377-wide window the Ideas board columns are 200 px.

**B6. Empty states don't teach.** Reports: ten tiles, all "no data" with no action. Measure: a table of dashes. Keywords: dashes. Content audit: a rose paragraph about no WordPress. Each should say the ONE thing to do next, as a button.

## C. Page-by-page friction (shorter)

- **Inbox**: three cards for the SAME article, each with three textareas — nine text boxes above the fold for one task. Group by article; one card, expandable.
- **Ideas**: `Write` is the primary button on a *video* idea (opens a script), `Approve` on an article idea — same column, different verbs. Chips truncate ("VIDEO · COMMUNITYFORCE SCHOLARSHIP & GRA…"). Reject/Delete both present. Priority explanation sentence ("Article priority shows its working on the card…") is a paragraph where a tooltip would do.
- **Drafts overview**: "Nothing being written right now" then a Scripts list — for an article-first tenant the page looks broken.
- **Review**: duplicate of Inbox (B1). Approvals tab correctly empty; Audit tab says "No WordPress connection" in red.
- **Publish**: good — the two held articles with Download HTML / Mark published; but the "-"-prefixed titles (A3).
- **Distribute**: good. The queue list is the clearest page in the app.
- **Measure**: dashes; "Set up analytics" is the only action and it's a text link. YouTube audit's connect card is fine.
- **Settings overview**: the "every dial under the question it answers" cards are good copy — but Publish Admin duplicates half of it (B1).
- **Automation**: dense but honest. The ideation/drafting/social/publishing mode row renders as raw tokens (`ideation:auto · blog_drafting:auto …`) in a code font.
- **Schedule**: fine. **Connections**: good list, but "Fix" buttons for optional things (HeyGen, Keyword volume) look like faults.
- **Brand**: colour fields empty (`#0d5a84` placeholder), Company info is a long rich-text; fine.
- **Publish Admin**: its own 10-tab strip, all-caps mono labels; Users duplicates People.
- **Help**: the best page — the guide, quick-starts, shortcuts. But it's a destination, not in context.
- **Article editor**: strong (stepper, 5 tabs, Gates sidebar) — but the hydration error (A2), and "Approve & publish" is primary on a CF post that has no WordPress (it will mark-published or fail?).
- **Compose / Calendar / Engage**: fine; Engage nags about dismissed reviews (A5).
- **Experts**: 12 auto-created SME profiles "just now" — the intake questions from the Inbox banked them; the page doesn't say these came from answers.
- **Thumbnails / Videos / Production / Scripts**: the video studio is on for CF because two YouTube channels exist; nothing in it is used. `/production` renders at ⅓ scale (A1).
- **Notifications**: 99+ unread; ⅓ scale (A1); no "mark all read".

## D. Recommendation — what the restart should do, in order

1. **Fix the defects first** (A1 zoom/scale, A2 hydration, A3 dash titles + repair the live post, A6 scripts SSR, A7 header chips → one plain-English status pill with a hover). Half a day, all verifiable in the browser.
2. **Collapse the duplicates (B1).** Review overview → redirect to Inbox (keep Approvals + Audit as Inbox tabs or under Publish). `/blog` vs `/blog/board` → one Articles page with a list/board toggle. Publish Admin → fold into Settings as more tabs (People, Automation, Schedule, Connections, **Keys**, **Analytics**, **Email**, **Workspace**, **Usage**); "Publish Admin" becomes the operator-only tab set, not a second rail entry. Remove the Ask drawer card from stage pages (the dock + Ctrl+/ stays; the header button stays).
3. **Make the rail reflect what a tenant does.** Hide the video studio tabs AND the video idea controls unless `studio:enabled` is explicitly on (today "absent = on" when a channel exists — flip the default to off; CF and LSI are blog+social tenants). Consider collapsing Research/Ideas/Drafts/Review/Publish into **Articles** (one page with a stepper: Ideas → Drafting → Review → Published) and keeping Distribute + Measure — the loop as a stepper inside one page, not seven rail entries. Prototype on Demo before renaming anything.
4. **Empty states with one action** (B6): each stage/tab gets a single sentence + one primary button when empty.
5. **Assistant page** (B4): replace the tool registry with 8–10 example asks grouped by intent; keep the registry behind "What can it do? →".
6. **Inbox cards** (C): group by entity; one card per article with its N questions collapsed; "Answer all" once.
7. **Help**: after the collapse, rewrite `lib/help.ts` "How is the Blog section organized?" (still describes the pre-September tab strip), the rail answer, the guide's stage sections, `knowledge.ts` APP_MAP_BRIEF, both SKILL.md copies, `help-tips.ts` for every moved route. Add a Help answer for the new status pill and for "why is a page tiny" once A1 is fixed (or drop it when fixed).

- Browser pane is signed in as the owner; `tabs_context` → tab `seed`. **Real tenants are read-only** — switch the workspace combobox to **Demo Workspace** before submitting anything.
- A1: navigate `/notifications` → screenshot; compare `document.documentElement.clientWidth` vs `document.body.getBoundingClientRect().width` and `getComputedStyle(document.body).zoom` via `javascript_tool`.
- A2: `read_console_messages` after `/blog/<id>` — expect zero #418 after the fix; find the node with `javascript_tool` by diffing SSR HTML (curl) against `document.body.innerHTML`.
- A3: DB probe for titles starting with `-` or a tab; the live CF post is "marked published" (no WP) — its `publishedUrl` is what to fix.
- Every collapse: hard-refresh open tabs after deploy (server-action ids change).
